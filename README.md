# 💩 Poop Pocket

A silly browser game by Kace. You've got an animal in your pocket. Squeeze it to shoot poop at people in the park!

## How to play

1. Download this folder and double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser.
2. **Click the park** to squeeze your animal and shoot poop where you clicked. The poop takes a moment to fly, so aim ahead of people who are walking.
3. **Out of poop?** Your animal gets hungry. Drag its food from the red bowl to its mouth to feed it and reload.
4. **Switch animals** with keys 1-9, 0, - and = or the buttons in your pocket.
   Your points turn into coins 🪙 after each game. Save them for the 🛒 shop (bottom of the start screen, or press S): the duck 🦆, bat 🦇, penguin 🐧, octopus 🐙, kangaroo 🦘 and dragon 🐉 are waiting!
   The shop also has 🎩 hats for your pocket animal (just for looks).
5. Each level gives you 40 seconds to reach a goal score. Make it and you go to the next level, where people walk faster and there are more of them. Runners 🏃 are worth 3, and hitting two people with one poop gives a bonus. Hit someone again within 2 seconds for a 🔥 combo: x2, then x3!
6. Watch out for sneaky people! They hop out of the way of small poops (bird, dog), but they can't escape the big splats (cow, elephant). They're worth 2, and there are more of them every level.
   People notice your animal too: some run from the elephant, some stop to stare at the unicorn (easy target!), and nobody likes the skunk smell.
   People with umbrellas ☂️ block your first poop, and skaters 🛹 zoom by fast (worth 3).
7. Every 2 levels you go somewhere new: 🌳 the park, then 🏖️ the beach, then 🏫 the school, then 🦁 the zoo, then 🎪 the carnival, then 🛍️ the mall, then 🚜 the farm, then 🌙 the moon (low gravity: floaty poops!), then 🐠 the reef (poops sink slowly), then 🏔️ the mountain (poops roll downhill!), then 🌃 the city at night, then 🌴 the jungle, and then around again.
8. On levels 3, 6, 9 and so on, a giant boss 👑 walks by. Hit him 3 times for 10 points!
   Want more bosses? Tap 👑 Boss Rush (top left of the start screen, or press B): 60 seconds of giant bosses, one after another, each faster than the last.
   Just want to relax? Tap 🧘 Zen (or press Z): no clock, no goals, just splat for fun. A new place every 20 splats. Tap 🏠 Done when you are finished.
   Playing with a friend? Tap 👥 2 Players (or press T): take turns on the same screen, 30 seconds each, highest score wins.
9. Power-ups float down from the sky. Tap one to grab it: ⭐ double points, 💥 super poop (giant splats), or 🍱 full tummies (every animal reloaded).
10. Earn 🏆 achievements, like beating a boss or reaching the zoo (press A to see them). Tap 📊 Stats (or press I) for your lifetime stats: total splats, time played, favourite animal and more.
11. Every day there is a new 📅 daily challenge (like "Splat 3 skaters") worth 30 coins, and every week a 🏅 big challenge (like "Beat 3 giant bosses") worth 100 coins.

Tap **❓ Help** on the start screen (or press ?) for a how-to-play guide. Press **M** or tap the speaker to change the volume: 🔊 loud, 🔉 quiet, 🔇 off.  Press **H** or tap 🎉 for Party mode: silly hats and confetti! Press **P** or **Esc**, or tap ⏸️, to pause. While paused, tap 📸 Save photo (or press C) to save a picture of the game to your Downloads folder. The game also pauses by itself when you switch to another tab.

## The animals

| Animal | Eats | Poops | Splat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🐦 Bird | seeds 🌾 | 6 | small and fast |
| 🐶 Dog | bones 🦴 | 4 | medium |
| 🐮 Cow | grass 🌿 | 3 | big |
| 🐘 Elephant | peanuts 🥜 | 2 | huge and slow |
| 🐒 Monkey | bananas 🍌 | 3 | throws 3 small poops at once |
| 🦄 Unicorn | strawberries 🍓 | 2 | magic poop that follows the person nearest your click |
| 🦨 Skunk | bugs 🐛 | 2 | leaves a stink cloud that splats anyone who walks into it |
| 🦆 Duck (shop, 60 🪙) | bread 🍞 | 3 | poop bounces to a second spot, like skipping a stone |
| 🦇 Bat (shop, 100 🪙) | mosquitoes 🦟 | 2 | swoops over and drops a line of 4 poops from the sky |
| 🐧 Penguin (shop, 150 🪙) | fish 🐟 | 2 | ice poop freezes everyone nearby for 3 seconds |
| 🐙 Octopus (shop, 200 🪙) | shrimp 🦐 | 2 | poop splits into 4 mini poops that splash outward |
| 🦘 Kangaroo (shop, 250 🪙) | leaves 🍃 | 2 | boomerang poop: splats, then swoops back and splats again |
| 🐉 Dragon (shop, 300 🪙) | peppers 🌶️ | 2 | chain poop: after a hit, fiery poops jump to 2 more people nearby |

Want to change how strong they are? Open `index.html` and look for `ANIMALS` near the top.
